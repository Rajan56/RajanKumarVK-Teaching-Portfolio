# Teaching Portfolio · Rajan Kumar V K, D.Sc. (Tech.)

**Learning is insight, not accumulation.** An interactive teaching portfolio: teaching philosophy, university teaching and mentoring, course design, sustainability in teaching, a planned master's course in sustainable business and a teaching case.

[**Open the live portfolio**](https://rajan56.github.io/RajanKumarVK-Teaching-Portfolio/)

## What is on the page

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Teaching philosophy | Questions before answers, relevance, student-centred learning, and insight rather than accumulation |
| Experience | Introduction to Industrial Engineering and Management and the Lahti Venture Program at LUT University (2022 to 2026); science and chemistry teaching in Nepal (2007 to 2012); university pedagogy studies |
| Sustainable business course | A planned master's course with interactive exercises: [Sustainability-in-Business-Teaching-Demo](https://rajan56.github.io/Sustainability-in-Business-Teaching-Demo/) |
| LUT teaching | Three classroom tools recreated: process bottleneck, team-role reflection, prioritisation game |
| Course design | A constructively aligned redesign of a first-year course |
| Mentoring | Guiding multidisciplinary student teams on company challenges |
| Sustainability | The triple bottom line in every case, with three teaching designs |
| Teaching case | Margin recovery: from data to decision, with energy and CO2 as part of performance (fictional data) |
| Videos | Two short narrated videos (synthetic voice, captions on screen) |

## Run locally

No build step. Open index.html, or serve the folder: python -m http.server 8000.
